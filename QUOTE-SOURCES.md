# Quote sources

Attributions supplied by Guillermo. These record where he encountered the phrases; they have not been independently verified as original authorship. The homepage displays plain-text credits without links.

1. “Remember that the darkest time is before dawn” — Fernando Atrisco
2. “The way the potential transforms into actuality is through discipline” — Jordan Peterson
3. “When it feels scary to jump that is exactly when you jump” — A Most Violent Year
4. “The magic you are looking for is in the job you're avoiding” — Chris Williamson
5. “It is forbidden not to fight for what you want” — Inscription on a grave in Venezuela
6. “The pain of doing nothing will always be worse than the pain of doing something” — Sleepy Charlie
7. “Sometimes those we least imagine are the ones who do what no one would imagine” — The Imitation Game
8. “It does not matter what we expect from life, but what life expects from us” — Viktor E. Frankl
9. “I fear only one thing: not being worthy of my sufferings” — Fyodor Dostoevsky
10. “Seal me in your heart..., because love is as strong as death” — Song of Solomon 8:6
11. “Suffering, in a way, ceases to be suffering when it finds meaning” — Viktor E. Frankl
12. “The question about Man does not refer to who we are, but to who we could be” — Jordan Peterson
13. “No tree can grow to heaven unless its roots reach hell” — Carl Jung
14. “Whoever has a why to live will almost always find the how” — Friedrich Nietzsche
15. “We accept the love we believe we deserve” — Stephen Chbosky
16. “To cure the soul by means of the senses, and the senses by means of the soul.” — Oscar Wilde
17. “I didn't fail, I just discovered 999 ways on how not to make a light bulb” — Thomas Edison
18. “Before you give up, remember that you are ending of ways to make mistakes” — Atherion
19. “Sometimes doing something leads to nothing” — Francis Alÿs
20. “I prefer not to have talent, but to have desire... I have urge, desire, hunger” — Jenny Arias
21. “If you always do what you've always done, you always get what you've always gotten” — Jessie Potter
22. “When in doubt, follow your curiosity. It never lies, and it knows more than you do about what's worth paying attention to” — Paul Graham

Source notes:

- 5: Guillermo encountered this inscription on a grave in a cemetery in Venezuela; author unknown.
- 6: Sleepy Charlie, https://www.youtube.com/@sleepycharliee
- 8 and 11: Viktor E. Frankl, *Man’s Search for Meaning* (1946).
- 16: The original wording was “Healing the soul through the senses and the senses through the soul”; the homepage uses the corrected Oscar Wilde wording.
- 18: Atherion, https://www.youtube.com/@atherion
- 19: Francis Alÿs, *Paradox of Praxis 1*.

Editorial note: Homepage punctuation and a few awkward English translations have been lightly edited for readability; the list above preserves the supplied wording. These are not verified verbatim quotations.
